How a job moves

From the file the customer sent to a sheet the bindery can cut

LockStep is the pace of a shop that already knows the next step. The software is named for that, not for a cloud queue.

  1. Land the file

    The PDF stays on the prepress PC or the shop server. Nothing has to upload before you can work.

  2. Fit the page

    Resize, crop, and rotate until the art matches the stock and the box you will actually cut.

  3. Impose

    Monkey, Grid, or Booklet. Step a card, gang a sheet, or paginate a saddle-stitch job with creep.

  4. Mark it

    Cutter puts L marks, registration, and dots where finishing expects them. Black or white.
