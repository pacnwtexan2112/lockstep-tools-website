Words and a picture on label stock.

LockStep Label lays out words and a picture on real label stock. It writes a PDF, or it prints to a local printer. It opens from the Labelmaker button in LockStepPDF, in its own window titled LockStep Label. It is not a tab inside LockStepPDF. A standalone copy comes later.

How you use it

Stock, words, a picture, then a PDF or a print.

Pick the sheet. Place the words and the picture. Read them on the sheet preview. Write a PDF, or print to a printer on this computer.

  • Stock

    Brand sheets include Avery, and Avery 5160. You can also build a custom sheet.

    A custom sheet is a rectangle, a circle, or a triangle. Arrange the labels as an even grid, or place each label. A rectangle can have a corner radius.

    Switch from an even grid to place each, and the words stay in the same place on each label.

  • Words

    Text can be many lines. Each line can go on every label, or only on the labels you click on the sheet preview. Set the font, the size, and the angle.

  • Picture

    Place a picture on the label. Resize it. Rotate it.

  • Sheet preview

    The sheet preview shows the labels on the stock. Click a label to put a line on that label only.

  • PDF and print

    Write a PDF of the sheet, or send the sheet to a local printer.

Before a full run

Print at actual size.

Print at actual size. Turn off fit-to-page.

Check the sheet against the physical stock before a full run.

Shared with LockStepPDF

Units and themes.

Units are inches, millimeters, or points. The unit is shared with LockStepPDF settings.

Themes match LockStepPDF: Tech Theme, Ink Wash, California Beaches, and Cobalt Sky.

Start over

Clear.

Clear resets the words, the picture, the stock, and the placement.
