Start

LockStepPDF — start here

The window you see the first time you open the program.

The screen is four panes. Drag a pane title onto another pane to swap them. Drag a pane edge to resize it. The hint under the Tools title says the same thing.

  1. Assets Manager, on the left. This is the list of files you are working on.
  2. Tools, in the middle. The buttons across the top open one tool at a time. The form under the buttons is that tool.
  3. Real-Time Monitor, on the right. This is the page you are looking at, with rulers, zoom, and a measure tool.
  4. Stack Steps, under the tools. This is the recipe. Each line is one thing the program will do when you build the final PDF.

A star on a tool name means that tool asks for a Pro license. Free tools open without one. Global Settings, Account, shows which is which.

The first job

  1. Click Add Files in Assets, or drop files onto the list.
  2. Open a document with the arrow beside its name, then click a page. The monitor shows that page.
  3. Open Page controls under the asset list when you want to change selected pages (crop, rotate, resize, color, bleed, a blank, or delete).
  4. Click a tool button when you want a step that runs on the whole job (multi-up, booklet, crop marks, mail merge, and the rest).
  5. On a tool form, fill in the numbers, then click the green Add button. The new line appears in Stack Steps. The monitor updates to that step.
  6. When the monitor looks right, click Build & Save Final PDF in the header.

Files you can add

PDF. PNG, JPG, and TIFF.

Word (.doc and .docx), PowerPoint (.ppt and .pptx), and Excel (.xls and .xlsx) are converted to PDF as they are added.

Excel asks first. Use each sheet's page setup, or Fit all columns on one page, and choose the sheets.

A JSON file dropped on the window loads a recipe. It does not become a page in the list.

Numbers

Every size, margin, gutter, bleed, and mark uses the unit in Global Settings: inches, millimeters, or points. Change the unit there and the numbers already on the forms convert. A comma or a period both work as a decimal.

The header

Build & Save Final PDF opens the save window. That is the file you keep.

Export Proofs opens the proof form. Choose Monitor, Selection, or Tree, then PDF, PNG, or JPG. A stamp and fold lines are optional. It does not write the production file.

Additional Tools opens Automation, LockStepOCR, and LockStepLabel.

Global Settings is units, theme, page-size presets, recipe folder, production folders, and the ruler color.

About is the version.

The left and right arrows undo and redo a change you made to the asset list (move, copy, delete, rotate, and the other page edits). Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y do the same. Mail Merge has its own undo for placing and deleting fields.

The tool bar

Drag a tool button to put it where you want it.

The triangle at the left of the bar shows every tool, or only the one you are using.

The small square at the right pops the tool buttons into their own window. Dock puts them back.

A tool that is taller than the pane scrolls. The mouse wheel scrolls the pane under the pointer.

Two ways a change gets saved into the job

Apply, on a Page controls popup, changes the pages you picked. Those edits collect in the Page level steps list under Page controls. Add to recipe turns that list into one Page setup line on the stack.

Add step, the green button on a tool form, adds a recipe line. That line runs when you build, on the files that are loaded at that moment. Overwrite replaces the stack line you have selected, when it is the same kind of step.

The original file you opened stays on disk. Clear in Assets drops the files from this job. It does not delete them from the folder they came from.

The job

Assets Manager

The file list on the left. Every other tool reads the pages that are loaded here.

Load a job

Add Files opens a file window. You can also drop PDFs onto the list or onto the main window.

Clear takes every file out of this job. The files on disk stay where they were.

A document with more than one page has an arrow. Click it to show the pages. Click it again to hide them. Right-click a file name for Show pages or Hide pages.

Highlight pages

Click a page to highlight it. Ctrl-click adds or removes one page. Shift-click highlights a run.

The monitor follows the last page you highlighted.

Page controls under the list act on the highlight: All, None, Odd, Even, Select by spec, Sizes, and Invert. Those are written up in Page controls.

Move and copy

Drag a page up or down. A navy line shows where it will land.

Drop it on another page and the program asks Move or Copy.

Move takes the page out of the document it came from. Copy leaves that document as it is and adds a duplicate.

Drop onto a document name and the program asks whether the page goes at the beginning or the end of that document.

The header undo arrow (and Ctrl+Z) steps back through these changes. The redo arrow (and Ctrl+Y) puts them forward again.

Right-click a page

Copy and Cut remember the page. Paste is on the menu where a paste is legal.

Rotate 90° clockwise, and Rotate 90° counter-clockwise, turn the highlighted pages now.

Resize this page… opens Resize.

Insert blank page after adds one blank after each highlighted page.

Delete this page removes the highlighted pages from the job. The file on disk is unchanged.

Single sided job and Double sided job mark the document so the Side column can show Front and Back.

Blank back for this sheet inserts an empty back. Use next page as the back pairs this page with the following one.

Always on front and Always on back pin the page to that side of a duplex sheet. A blank is inserted in front when that is what it takes to land the page on the pinned side. Clear side removes the pin. The Lock column shows the pin.

Join selected pages and Split selected pages are the same actions as in Rearrange. They write a new PDF from the highlight.

Right-click a file name

Show pages or Hide pages.

Insert slip sheet between selected documents, when more than one document can take a slip.

Delete this file, or Delete selected files when several names are highlighted. This removes them from the job.

Single sided job and Double sided job.

The columns

File is the document name. A page row shows the page number. A note in parentheses, such as (Brochure p2), is the page's home file and original page. That note stays when you move the page. It is cleared when you renumber.

Specs, at the bottom, chooses which property columns are visible and their order. Check a column to show it. Move left and Move right set the order. Drag a column edge in the list to widen it.

The columns are Size, Width, Height, Color, Orientation, Side, Lock, Rotation, and Source page. Size, Width, and Height use the unit from Global Settings. Color is Color or B&W. Orientation is Portrait or Landscape. Side is Front or Back once the document is marked single or double sided.

Sizes, in Page controls, lists the sizes already in the job. Check one or more and every page of those sizes is highlighted.

The bottom bar

Specs opens the column chooser.

The recycle button renumbers. It warns first. Pages are numbered again from the top of each document. A nested document becomes part of the file around it. The old page notes in parentheses are removed. The word copy is removed from the document name. Continue does it. Cancel leaves the list alone.

Print opens the highlighted pages and documents in your PDF viewer, from top to bottom. Clear the highlight first if you want the whole list. Print from that viewer at actual size, with fit-to-page turned off. The loaded files are not saved by this.

A file that does not allow changes can still be shown. Build & Save reports that the file does not allow changes, and the working view stays on screen for display.

The job

Real-Time Monitor

The page on the right. It shows the step you have selected in the stack, or the loaded page when the stack is empty.

What the picture is

The status line says what you are viewing, such as the original page or the name of a step.

The monitor draws the visible page. A larger Media box can exist while the picture stays on the crop. Bleed explains how to open that hidden band so it shows here.

Rulers follow the same visible page.

Move through the job

File and the two file buttons step between loaded documents. The label reads File: current / total.

Page and the two page buttons step inside the current document. The label reads Page: current / total.

Clicking a page in Assets jumps the monitor to that page.

Print

Print opens the sheets on this monitor in the default PDF viewer, including imposed changes that are not saved yet. Print from that viewer at actual size, with fit-to-page turned off.

Ctrl-click or Shift-click imposed sheets to open only that group. With no group, every sheet is included. While the monitor is showing the loaded files, the asset highlight is what opens.

F5

The F5 button, and the F5 key, redraw the monitor from the asset list and the settings already on screen. The list, the recipe, the form, and the page you are on stay as they are. Use it when the picture looks stuck.

Preview Merged

Off, you see one file at a time.

On, the loaded files are shown as one run.

Show Rulers

Turn this on for tick marks along the top and the side. Hover the page and the readout is the distance from the left edge and the top edge, in the current unit.

The ruler color is in Global Settings. Tick marks and numbers switch between light and dark so they stay readable.

Measure

Right-click the page and choose Measure from here.

Move to the other point and right-click again. The distance stays on the page.

Click a measurement, then press Delete or Backspace, to remove it. Escape drops a measure you have started and not finished.

Measuring works while the rulers are on.

View

1 page fills the monitor with the current page.

2 columns shows a pair side by side. Use this to check a duplex pair or a booklet spread.

Thumbnails shows a small grid of pages. Zoom still applies.

Zoom

The menu is 1.0x (Fit Width), Fit Height, 1.5x Zoom, 2.0x High Zoom, and 3.0x Max Detail.

Minus zooms out one step. Plus zooms in one step.

Scrollbars appear when the page is larger than the pane. The wheel scrolls the pane under the pointer.

Pop out

The small square at the right of the monitor bar opens the monitor in its own window. Dock, in that window, puts it back.

While it is popped out, the main window shows a Dock strip in the monitor's old place.

Booklet

When a booklet step is selected, a banner can appear above the picture. Page forward to inner sheets to see creep. Booklet covers that.

The job

Stack Steps

The recipe. Each line is one action. Build & Save runs the lines from top to bottom on the files loaded at that moment.

Add a line

Open a tool, set the form, and click the green Add button (Add Grid Imposition, Add Booklet Step, Add to Stack, and the others). The new line is selected. The monitor shows the result of the stack up through that line.

Double-click a line to open its tool with those settings filled in.

Overwrite

Select a line, change the form, and click Overwrite. The selected line must be the same kind of step. Overwrite replaces that line. Add would insert another one.

The buttons beside the list

Move Up and Move Down change the order. Order matters. A crop that runs before a resize is a different job from a resize that runs first.

Remove takes out the selected line. The monitor rebuilds without it.

Reset clears the whole stack. It leaves the loaded files and the form as they are.

Save Recipe writes the stack to a JSON file you name.

Load Recipe reads a JSON file back onto the stack.

Recipes menu

This list is the folder you chose in Global Settings, Recipe folder. Pick a name to load it. None clears the loaded recipe.

The hint under the menu says to choose a folder when none is set. Files you drop into that folder show up here. Files you remove from the folder drop off.

Production destination

This menu is the folders you added in Global Settings, Production destinations. After you save, a copy of the finished PDF also goes into the folder you pick here. The file you save stays where you chose to save it. None skips the copy.

The list itself

Hover a line that is cut off and a tip shows the full text.

Click a line to view the job as of that step. The monitor status names it.

You can drop a recipe JSON file onto the list to load it.

Page setup

Page edits you Apply from Page controls can be gathered into one stack line named Page setup. That line is the page-level list, stored with the recipe. Add to recipe, under Page level steps, creates it. The recipe still runs on the files that are loaded when you run it.

What a line remembers

A line stores the numbers that were on the form, including the unit they were entered in. Loading a recipe onto a job that uses a different unit converts the lengths.

A page filter on the line (all, odd, even, or a list such as 1, 3, 5 or 1-4) limits which pages that line touches.

The job

Page controls

The row under the asset list. It starts closed. Click Page controls to open it. Click it again to close it.

Nothing in this row is active until a document is loaded. Rotate, Resize, Blank, and Delete also wait until a page is highlighted. Color, Crop, and Bleed/Box open their tools and can apply to a wider set of pages.

Select pages first

All highlights every page in the loaded documents.

None clears the highlight. Other documents stay unhighlighted too.

Odd highlights odd pages (fronts, if you count from 1).

Even highlights even pages (backs).

Select by spec opens a window titled Select by spec. Each row is one column from the asset list (Size, Width, Height, Color, Orientation, Side, Lock, Rotation, Source page). Check every property that must match, pick the value, and click Select. A page is highlighted only when it matches all of the checked properties. The window says Match every checked property. If nothing matches, you get "No pages match that selection."

Sizes is a menu of the sizes already in the job. Check one or more. Every page of those sizes is selected.

Invert swaps the highlight inside the current document. Other documents stay as they are.

Open a tool

Color, Crop, Bleed/Box, Rotate, and Resize each open a window with that tool's form. The Tools pane stays on whatever tool you already had open. The open button is the active face. The others wait.

The form in the window is the same form described in that tool's walkthrough: Color, Crop, Bleed, Rotate, and Resize.

Each of those windows has two ways to commit:

Apply, in the footer, with Apply to set to Selection, This document, or Entire tree.

Selection uses the highlighted pages.

This document uses every page of the document you are in.

Entire tree uses every page of every loaded file.

Apply writes a page-level edit. The pages rebuild, and the edit appears in Page level steps. The file you originally opened on disk is left as it was.

Add step (Add Crop Step, Add Resize Step, and the matching buttons) puts a recipe line on Stack Steps. That line uses the Pages Selector Filter on the form: all, odd/front, even/back, or a typed list. It runs later, when you build, on the files loaded at that time. Overwrite replaces the selected stack line of the same type.

Blank

Blank opens Insert blank page. It does not open the Booklet tool.

Insert blank page at is End of Document, Beginning of Document, or After Page #.

The small box beside After Page # is the page number. It accepts one page, a list such as 1, 3, 5, or a range such as 1-4.

Pages Selector Filter and the All, Odd / Front, and Even / Back buttons fill that filter.

Apply to selected pages inserts the blank. There is no Selection / document / tree choice on Blank. Highlight the pages first.

Delete

Delete removes each highlighted page from the job. The file you loaded is not changed on disk. Delete and Backspace do the same when the asset list has focus.

Page level steps

This list is every Apply you have made and not yet cleared: a rotate, a resize, a crop, a color change, a bleed, a blank.

Click a line to select it. Delete, or Remove step, takes that line out and rebuilds the pages without it. When nothing is highlighted, Remove step takes the last line.

Right-click a line for Remove this step or Reset page level steps.

Reset steps clears the whole list and puts the pages back. The recipe stack is left alone.

Add to recipe adds these page edits to the stack as one Page setup line.

The note under the buttons says the recipe runs on the files that are loaded when you run it.

The same buttons exist inside Rearrange, on the list of pages in the loaded PDFs. They call these same tools.

The job

Build and Save

Write the finished PDF. The button in the header is Build & Save Final PDF.

Before the window opens

Load at least one file. An empty job says No input files loaded.

If you placed Mail Merge fields and have not clicked Add to Stack, the program asks whether to add that step. Yes adds it and continues. No returns you to the job so you can add it yourself.

File Handling Options

Merge all assets into a single PDF is the starting choice. You then pick one file name. The suggested name is the first file's name plus _locksteppdf.

Export as individual PDFs writes one PDF per loaded file into a folder you pick.

Append Suffix appears for individual PDFs. That text is added to the end of each original file name.

File Size & Compression

Maximum Stream Deflation & Font Deduplication compresses content and strips unused duplicate fonts.

Optimize & Compress Image Streams flattens uncompressed images into smaller JPEG or PNG streams.

Max resolution is Original, 300, 600, 1200, or a number you type.

Original sends the file's own pixels, including a 1200 DPI image and private image data from Illustrator.

A number downsamples only images that are above that resolution. Images already below the number are left alone.

A proof

Build & Save writes the production file with no stamp and no fold lines. A proof, with a stamp or fold lines, is Export Proofs.

Choose Path & Save

Pick the file, or the folder, and the build runs. The header shows Exporting while it works.

A page-geometry warning, when there is one, appears after the save.

A file that does not allow changes stops with that reason. The working view stays available.

Success names the file you wrote.

What gets built

The stack runs from top to bottom on the files that are loaded now.

A production destination, if you picked one on Stack Steps, also receives a copy. The file you named stays where you saved it.

Page Geometry, in Bleed/Box, is applied as the files are read, before the stack.

Page tools

Crop

Shave a margin off the pages you choose. Open it from Page controls, Crop. The same form is what Rearrange opens when you click Crop there.

A first crop

  1. Highlight the pages in Assets. Odd or Even is enough when every front, or every back, needs the same trim.
  2. Open Page controls and click Crop.
  3. Set Step to the nudge you want, or pick a preset.
  4. Type an amount on each edge, or click the arrows beside an edge. Each click adds or subtracts the step. An edge stops at zero.
  5. At the bottom of the window, set Apply to Selection, This document, or Entire tree, and click Apply.
  6. The monitor shows the smaller page. The edit is listed under Page level steps.

The amounts are in the unit from Global Settings. Up and Down on an edge box do the same as the arrows.

Step and Presets

Step is the amount one arrow adds or subtracts. Type any step.

Presets fills Step from a short list in the current unit.

Inches: 0.001, 0.01, 0.0625 (1/16), 0.125 (1/8), 0.25.

Millimeters: 0.1, 0.5, 1, 2, 5.

Points: 0.25, 1, 3, 6, 18.

The four edges

Top sits above Left and Right. Bottom sits under them.

Each edge has a number and two arrows. The up arrow adds the step. The down arrow subtracts it.

A zero edge is left alone. A page is cropped only on the edges that have an amount.

Pages Selector Filter

This is for the green Add Crop Step button, which puts a line on the stack.

Type all, odd, even, front, back, a list such as 1, 3, 5, or a range such as 1-4.

All, Odd / Front, and Even / Back write those words into the box.

Add Crop Step stores that filter on the stack line. Overwrite replaces the selected Crop line.

Apply and the stack

Apply changes the pages in the scope you picked and records a page-level step.

Add Crop Step records a recipe line that runs at build time.

Use Apply when you are trimming the pages in front of you. Use Add Crop Step when this trim should be part of a recipe you will run again.

Resize

Put each chosen page on a new width and height. Open it from Page controls, Resize. Rearrange's Resize button opens the same window.

A first resize

  1. Highlight a page so the percent box has something to measure.
  2. Open Page controls and click Resize.
  3. Pick a preset, type Width and Height, or type a percent of the focused page.
  4. Click a dot on the anchor pad for where the artwork sits. Type H and V if you want it nudged from that dot.
  5. Set Apply to Selection, This document, or Entire tree, and click Apply.

The artwork scales to fit the new page and keeps its proportions. The anchor places it. Offset moves it right and down from that point. Stretch fills the width and height exactly and can distort the art.

Presets, Width, Height, and percent

Presets is the page-size list from Global Settings, Custom Page Sizes. Picking one fills Width and Height. Those sizes are stored as portrait (short side by long side). Landscape is the checkbox, not a second preset.

Width and Height are in the current unit. A comma or a period is a decimal.

Percent sits beside them. Type 102 or 102% and press Enter, or click away. Width and Height become that percent of the focused page. Editing Width or Height by hand clears the percent, because the size is no longer "this percent."

Anchor and offset

The nine dots are the corners, the edge centers, and the center of the new page. The filled dot is the one in use. The question mark beside the pad explains it.

Offset H is horizontal, in the current unit. Positive moves the artwork right.

Offset V is vertical. Positive moves the artwork down.

Force Landscape Orientation Layout

Turn this on when the new page should be wider than it is tall. The preset stays the portrait pair. This checkbox swaps how it is used.

Stretch content layer to fit bounding box

Off, the art keeps its shape and may leave a margin on one axis.

On, the art is stretched to the exact width and height.

Pages Selector Filter

This filter is for Add Resize Step.

all, odd, even, front, back, a list such as 1, 3, 5, or a range such as 1-4.

All, Odd / Front, and Even / Back fill the box.

Add Resize Step puts that on the stack. Overwrite replaces the selected Resize line.

Apply, with Selection, This document, or Entire tree, writes a page-level step instead.

Related Page controls Rearrange Global Settings

Page tools

Rotate

Turn pages clockwise. Open it from Page controls, Rotate.

A first turn

  1. Highlight the pages, or plan to use This document or Entire tree.
  2. Open Page controls and click Rotate.
  3. Click 90°, 180°, or 270°. The number box fills in, and the button you clicked is marked.
  4. Set Apply to and click Apply.

90° turns clockwise and swaps width and height.

180° turns the page upside down. Width and height stay.

270° turns the other way and swaps width and height.

You can type another number of degrees. The 90, 180, and 270 buttons light only when the box is exactly one of those clockwise turns. Zero is treated as no choice.

Pages Selector Filter

all, odd, even, front, back, a list, or a range. All, Odd / Front, and Even / Back fill the box.

Odd is fronts. Even is backs. Use that for a duplex job where only one side lies the wrong way.

Add Rotation Step stores the filter on a stack line. Overwrite replaces the selected Rotate line.

Apply writes a page-level step for Selection, This document, or Entire tree.

A quicker turn

Right-click a highlighted page in Assets and choose Rotate 90° clockwise or Rotate 90° counter-clockwise. That applies immediately to the highlight. Counter-clockwise is the same turn as 270°.

Rearrange has Left and Right buttons that do the same turns on the pages selected in that window.

Page tools

Color

Change the color of the pages, and adjust tone, color, and ink. Color is a Pro tool. Open it from Page controls, Color. Preflight's Open Color (RGB fix) jumps here too.

A first conversion

  1. Highlight the pages, or plan the Apply scope.
  2. Open Page controls and click Color.
  3. Click a color space. The chosen one is the mint face.
  4. Pick a Target DPI.
  5. Move any sliders you need. The monitor updates as you move them.
  6. Click Apply. The same result is written into the page-level step. Add the step when you want it on the recipe instead.

Color space

Keep Original Document Colors leaves the space alone. Sliders can still change tone.

Grayscale (256 Levels) makes a gray image.

Black & White (High Contrast) makes a stark black and white.

Sepia warms the page.

CMYK (Commercial Print) is for a press.

RGB (Digital/Web) is for a screen.

Target DPI

300, 400, 600, or 1200. The conversion is rasterized at that resolution. A higher number is a larger file and a finer picture.

Tone

Brightness, Contrast, Highlights, Shadows, and Gamma.

Positive brightness, highlights, and shadows move toward paper white.

Gamma 1.00 leaves midtones in place. The slider runs from 0.20 to 3.00.

Chroma

Saturation, Vibrance, Hue, Cyan / Red, Magenta / Green, and Yellow / Blue.

Vibrance moves duller colors first.

On a balance slider, negative is cyan, magenta, or yellow. Positive is red, green, or blue.

Chroma is available for Keep Original, Grayscale, CMYK, and RGB. Black & White and Sepia leave chroma alone.

Ink (CMYK conversion)

These sliders apply when the space is CMYK.

Black replacement runs from 0 to 100. 100 sends neutral gray to black.

Total ink limit runs from 0 to 400. 0 means no cap. 280 and 320 are common press caps.

Cyan ink, Magenta ink, Yellow ink, and Black ink. Positive adds that channel. Negative reduces it.

Reset adjustments

Puts the sliders back to their start. The color space stays on what you picked.

The warning bar

A yellow bar appears when the choice needs a look before you commit, for example a conversion that will rasterize live type. Read it before you Apply.

Apply and the stack

Apply uses Selection, This document, or Entire tree, and records a page-level step. The Color column in Assets updates for those pages.

The green add button on the form records a Color Conversion line on the stack, using the Pages Selector Filter.

Overwrite replaces the selected Color line.

Sliders update the monitor immediately. The step is what writes the same result into the PDF you build.

Related Page controls Preflight

Page tools

Bleed

Open a bleed, paint one, or change how the page boxes are read. Open it from Page controls, Bleed/Box. The window title is Bleed/Box.

There are two jobs in this window. Page Geometry is how a loaded file is read. Bleeds is a step you add.

Page Geometry / Box Settings

Click the triangle to open it. The lines under the title are the Media, Crop, and Trim of the selected file, in the current unit. Load a file to read them. While they are being read, the line says Reading page size…

Do nothing (keep original boxes).

The boxes stay. The preview still lines the crop up with the media so the monitor and the rulers agree.

Set CropBox to match TrimBox.

The visible page becomes the trim. Artwork in the bleed is still in the file. The monitor hides it, because the monitor draws the crop.

Remove extra margins / white space.

The page is clipped to the trim. The bleed band is gone after this.

The box choice is how the loaded file is read before the stack. Growing a bleed does not change this choice. If you remove the extra margins first, a later Show bleed has no hidden art left to open.

Bleeds — paint a new margin

The amount is labeled Grow bleed by when a paint method is selected. It is added to every side. The page grows by twice that amount in each direction. The unit label beside the box is the current unit.

Quick grow (scale artwork to the larger page).

The art is enlarged until it covers the new page. The interior moves outward a little. This stays vector. Edge render DPI is off.

Edge extend: mirror the outer edge into the bleed.

The art stays at its size and position. The band just inside each edge is rendered and flipped outward. Use this for photos and backgrounds. Edge render DPI is the resolution of that band only. The art itself stays as it was.

Edge extend: stretch the last pixels outward.

The outermost row of pixels is pulled outward. Use this for solid colors and borders that already run to the edge. Edge render DPI is used here too.

The result writes TrimBox as the original page and BleedBox as the grown page. Grid and Booklet can then pack on the finished size when Source Trim is on, and crop marks land on the original edge.

Show bleed — open a band that is already there

Choose No bleed grow. The amount label changes to Show bleed.

Type a distance, such as 0.125. The monitor opens by that much on every side. Artwork that was already outside the cut line, and hidden by the page box, comes into view. The cut line stays where it was. Nothing is scaled and no new pixels are painted. A page with nothing in that band shows white there.

Show bleed already in the PDF (cut off by the Media, Crop, or Trim box).

This checkbox is available only with No bleed grow. Leave the amount at 0 and turn it on to open the whole bleed the file already has. A positive Show bleed amount wins and opens only that far, even if the checkbox is also on. Switching to a paint method clears the checkbox.

Apply to

All pages grows every page.

Selected pages uses the pages highlighted in Assets. Pages that are not highlighted keep their own size. If you add the step with Selected pages and nothing is highlighted, the program asks you to highlight the pages first.

Pages Selector Filter can name all, odd, even, or a list. A filter other than all is what the step stores.

Commit

Image Resolution (Min DPI). Flags a placed image that is below this resolution at final size. A common press number is 300. A quick-print number is 200.

Press & RIP Safety

Missing / Unembedded Fonts. Flags live text whose font is not embedded.

Flag RGB Content. Flags images, shapes, or text that are not CMYK.

Drag the picture on the label. Drag a corner to resize it. Drag the round handle, or type Angle, to turn it. The angle runs from -180 to 180.

That placement repeats on every label of the sheet.

4. First label

Row and Column are where the printing starts. Row 1, column 1 is the top-left label. Labels before that stay blank. Use this when the sheet is already partly used.

5. Nudge

Right and Down are in points, in half-point steps. Positive numbers move the printing right or down. Use this when a test sheet sits a little off the stock.

Application Theme

The menu is the installed themes. Picking one saves it, closes Settings, and rebuilds the window in that theme. Your job stays loaded.

Global Unit of Measurement

Inches (in), Millimeters (mm), or Points (pt).
